# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that makes the community better:

- Being patient and respectful, with beginners as with experts.
- Giving and accepting feedback on the work, not on the person, and assuming good faith.
- Admitting mistakes, and learning from them.
- Thinking of what is best for the community and the project, not only for oneself.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or images, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a home or an email address, without their permission.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## What the maintainers do

The maintainers explain the standards, and act when someone breaks them. They may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, pull requests and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and say why.

## Where it applies

In every space of the project: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere a person speaks
for the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a violation, write privately to the maintainers through [Mative's contact page](https://www.mative.ai/en/contact)
and say that it is about the code of conduct of ADSS. Every report is read, reviewed promptly and fairly,
and treated with respect for the privacy and the safety of the person who made it.

The maintainers answer in one of these ways, as the case asks:

1. **Correction:** a private, written note, with the reason, and sometimes an apology.
2. **Warning:** a consequence for continued behavior, with no interaction with the people involved for a set time.
3. **Temporary ban:** no interaction or public communication with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, or for harassment of a person or a group.
